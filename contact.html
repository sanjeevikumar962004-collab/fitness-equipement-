COMMERCIAL FAQ

SPEAK WITH AN ENGINEER

Do you provide complimentary 3D facility CAD modeling?

Yes. Send us your floor dimensions or architect blueprint. Within 48 hours, our drafting team produces rendered 3D CAD layouts showing exact rig footprint, clearance corridors, and plate storage zoning.

Can our gym cages be custom powder-coated in our brand colors?

Absolutely. We operate our own in-house electrostatic powder coating line. We match RAL color codes and can laser-cut your facility's insignia directly into the crossmembers and nameplates.

How does shipping and professional installation work?

We palletize all steel components in wooden shipping crates and ship via dedicated logistics carriers throughout India. For large facilities, our Salem installation crew travels on-site for bolt-down commissioning.

Can I test equipment before placing a commercial order?

Yes. Our Salem Showroom on Meyyanur Bypass Road is fully outfitted with all power cages, barbells, and machines. Contact us or select "Showroom Visit" on the inquiry form to book an appointment.
