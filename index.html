FAQ

DO YOU SHIP ACROSS INDIA?

Yes, we provide pan-India shipping for all our equipment. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on weight and location. For large commercial orders, we arrange dedicated freight transport.

WHAT ARE THE WARRANTY TERMS?

Our lifetime structural warranty covers all steel frames and welds against structural failure under normal use. Upholstery, cables, and moving parts have separate warranty periods ranging from 1 to 3 years.

DO YOU PROVIDE INSTALLATION SERVICES?

We offer professional installation services within Tamil Nadu for commercial setups and large home gym packages. Most of our residential equipment includes detailed assembly instructions for self-assembly.

CAN I VISIT YOUR SHOWROOM IN SALEM?

Absolutely. Our Salem headquarters features a full showroom where you can test our equipment before purchasing. Contact our sales team to schedule a visit.
